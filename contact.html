Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about driver requirements, insurance, and booking policies.

You will need a valid driver's license held for at least 1 year, a government-issued photo ID or passport, and a major credit card or debit card in the primary driver's name. International drivers must present an International Driving Permit (IDP) alongside their national license.

The standard minimum age to rent with DriveEase is 21 years old. Drivers aged 21-24 may incur a nominal young driver surcharge depending on the vehicle category. Luxury and high-performance supercars require drivers to be at least 25 years old.

DriveEase offers 100% free cancellations on all standard reservations up to 24 hours prior to your scheduled pickup time. No cancellation fee will be charged and any pre-paid amounts will be fully refunded to your original payment method.

All rentals come standard with basic third-party liability coverage and 24/7 nationwide roadside assistance. During checkout, you can opt for additional Full Collision Damage Waiver (CDW) protection for maximum peace of mind.

All electric vehicles (such as Tesla and Porsche Taycan) are delivered with at least 80% charge. You will be provided with an EV charging network adapter card for access to nationwide Superchargers and public charging stations.
