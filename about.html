Our Legacy

Redefining Car Rental Experiences Worldwide

Founded in 2011, DriveEase was created with a simple vision: to eliminate the friction, hidden fees, and hassle traditionally associated with car rentals.

Over the last 15 years, we have grown from a local fleet of 20 vehicles to a nationwide network operating over 500 premium, luxury, and electric vehicles across 50+ major airport hubs and metropolitan downtown centers.
